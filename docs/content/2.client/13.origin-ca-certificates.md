## Create

Create an Origin CA certificate.

::params-table
---
params:
  - name: "values"
    type: "array"
    required: true
    description: "`csr`, `hostnames` and `request_type` (`origin-rsa`, `origin-ecc` or `keyless-certificate`) are required. `requested_validity` is the certificate's lifetime in days, defaulting to Cloudflare's own."
